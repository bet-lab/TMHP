Demand & weather¶

Outdoor-air temperature utilities and domestic hot water demand profiles consumed by the dynamic simulation entry points.

Weather¶

Weather data processing and irradiance calculations.

Irradiance convention of the dynamic models¶

The I_DN_schedule / I_dH_schedule inputs of the boiler models are the direct-normal irradiance (DNI, beam on a surface facing the sun) and the diffuse-horizontal irradiance (DHI), step averages in W/m². A collector or PV array turns them into the irradiance on its own plane with plane_of_array_irradiance(), from the sun position at the step midpoint (solar_position(), site and clock from the collector’s latitude, longitude, utc_offset_h and start_time). Measured global-horizontal irradiance (GHI, e.g. KMA data) is split into DNI and DHI with decompose_ghi_to_poa(), whose result carries dni and dhi columns.

tmhp.weather.plane_of_array_irradiance(dni, dhi, *, zenith, azimuth, tilt, surface_azimuth, albedo=0.2, sky_model='perez', day_of_year=79.0)[source]¶

Irradiance on a tilted plane [W/m²] from DNI and DHI and the sun position.

Beam DNI·cos θ (zero with the sun below the horizon or behind the plane), sky diffuse from sky_model ("perez" (default, as decompose_ghi_to_poa()), "haydavies" or "isotropic", via pvlib) and ground-reflected albedo·GHI·(1 − cos β)/2 with GHI = DNI·cos z + DHI. With the sun below the horizon the beam is zero and the sky diffuse is isotropic.

Returns beam, sky, ground, total, aoi [deg], ghi, zenith and dni_used (the DNI after the horizon check).

Parameters:

  • zenith (float)

  • azimuth (float)

  • tilt (float)

  • surface_azimuth (float)

  • albedo (float)

  • sky_model (str)

  • day_of_year (float)

tmhp.weather.solar_position(day_of_year, *, latitude=37.57, longitude=126.98, utc_offset_h=9.0)[source]¶

Solar zenith and azimuth [deg] at a fractional day of year (local standard time).

day_of_year counts from 1 at 1 January 00:00 local standard time (utc_offset_h hours ahead of UTC); its fraction is the clock time. Spencer (1971) declination and equation of time with the analytical zenith/azimuth (Duffie & Beckman, Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes, 4th ed., §1.6): within 0.3° (zenith) and 0.4° (azimuth) of NREL SPA over a year at Seoul, without atmospheric refraction. Azimuth is clockwise from north (180 = south), as in pvlib.

Parameters:

  • day_of_year (float)

  • latitude (float)

  • longitude (float)

  • utc_offset_h (float)

Return type:

tuple[float, float]

Domestic hot water demand¶

Domestic Hot Water (DHW) load modeling.

Besides the draw-schedule helpers, this module holds the hot-water service accounting the boiler models share: what a step was asked to deliver, what it did not deliver (dhw_service_columns()), the totals of a run (summarize_dhw_service()) and the end-of-run warning (warn_if_dhw_unmet()).

tmhp.dhw.DHW_REQUEST_KEY: str = 'Q_dhw_request [W]'¶

hot-water heat a step was asked to deliver [W].

Type:

Result key

tmhp.dhw.DHW_SETPOINT_TOL_K: float = 0.01¶

Width of the service set point [K].

A delivery whose shortfall is no more than the requested flow cooled by this much counts as met (Q_dhw_unmet [W] is 0). The mixing valve of a multi-node tank is set on the outlet temperature by iteration (to 1e-3 K), and a lumped tank sitting on the set point differs between platforms in the last digits; neither is a hot-water shortfall. 0.01 K is 0.04 % of a 25 K lift.

tmhp.dhw.calc_cold_water_temp(df, target_date_str, *, latitude=None)[source]¶

Mains water temperature [°C] by the EnergyPlus Site:WaterMainsTemperature correlation.

The correlation of Burch & Christensen (NREL; Hendron et al. 2004) as EnergyPlus evaluates it (Engineering Reference, “Water Mains Temperatures”; WaterMainsTempFromCorrelation in WeatherManager.cc). It is fitted in °F, so the inputs are converted to °F, the correlation is evaluated there and the result converted back:

T_mains = (T_avg + 6) + ratio · (ΔT_max / 2) · sin(0.986·(day − 15 − lag) − 90)
ratio   = 0.4 + 0.01·(T_avg − 44)
lag     = 35 − 1.0·(T_avg − 44)

in °F with the sine argument in degrees, T_avg the annual mean outdoor temperature and ΔT_max the spread (max − min) of the monthly mean outdoor temperatures. The result is not allowed below 32 °F (0 °C). South of the equator the seasonal term changes sign, as in EnergyPlus.

References

Hendron, R., Anderson, R., Christensen, C., Eastment, M., & Reeves, P. (2004). Development of an Energy Savings Benchmark for All Residential End-Uses. SimBuild 2004, IBPSA-USA. EnergyPlus Engineering Reference, Water Systems.

Parameters:

  • df (DataFrame) – The twelve monthly mean outdoor temperatures [°C] in a column whose name contains T_avg (or 기온, temp, T0, °C, ℃).

  • target_date_str (str) – Target date string in ‘YYYY-MM-DD’ format.

  • latitude (float | None) – Site latitude [°]; negative flips the season. None: northern hemisphere.

Returns:

Mains water temperature [°C].

Return type:

float

tmhp.dhw.dhw_service_columns(dV_request, dV_delivered, T_delivered_K, T_set_K, T_sup_w_K)[source]¶

Hot-water heat a step was asked for and the part it did not deliver.

The request is the scheduled mixed-water flow at the service set point, counted from the mains temperature:

Q_dhw_request = rho·c · dV_request · (T_set − T_sup_w)

The delivery is the mixed water that actually left the valve at the temperature it left at, rho·c · dV_delivered · (T_delivered − T_sup_w), and Q_dhw_unmet = Q_dhw_request − delivery (never negative). Two things make it positive:

  • the tank is colder than the set point, so the mixing valve passes tank water unmixed and the water arrives below the set point;

  • a tank whose level varies stopped the draw at its lowest level, so less water was delivered (dV_mix_w_out_unmet [m3/s]).

Both are step averages like every other rate in a dynamic result: Q_dhw_unmet·dt is the step’s shortfall in joules. A shortfall within DHW_SETPOINT_TOL_K of the set point is reported as 0.

Parameters:

  • dV_request (float) – Mixed-water flow the draw schedule asks for [m³/s].

  • dV_delivered (float) – Mixed-water flow that left the mixing valve [m³/s].

  • T_delivered_K (float) – Temperature of the delivered water [K]; unused when nothing was delivered.

  • T_set_K (float) – Service set point of the mixing valve [K].

  • T_sup_w_K (float) – Mains water temperature [K].

Returns:

{"Q_dhw_request [W]": ..., "Q_dhw_unmet [W]": ...}. Both are 0 on a step without a draw, and when the mains water is already at the set point (nothing to heat).

Return type:

dict[str, float]
