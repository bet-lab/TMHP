Refrigerant & thermodynamics¶

CoolProp-backed state-point helpers, cycle-level analysis (compression ratio, isentropic efficiency, COP), compressor operating-envelope guards, and COP correlations.

Refrigerant state points¶

Refrigerant cycle calculations and optimization.

tmhp.refrigerant.calc_ref_state(T_evap_K, T_cond_K, refrigerant, eta_cmp_isen, mode='heating', dT_superheat=0.0, dT_subcool=0.0, is_active=True, rps=None, x_suction=1.0)[source]¶

Compute the four thermodynamic state points of the refrigerant cycle.

The vapour-compression cycle has four canonical state points:

  • State 1 (cmp_in): compressor inlet — low-pressure superheated vapour at the evaporator outlet.

  • State 2 (cmp_out): compressor outlet — high-pressure superheated vapour at the condenser inlet.

  • State 3 (exp_in): expansion-valve inlet — high-pressure subcooled liquid at the condenser outlet.

  • State 4 (exp_out): expansion-valve outlet — low-pressure two-phase mixture at the evaporator inlet.

Keys in the returned dict are always assigned by the physical compressor / expander ports; the mode argument is preserved verbatim under the "mode" key but does not change the key naming.

Wet suction¶

x_suction < 1 puts State 1 inside the two-phase dome at the evaporating pressure (the evaporator outlet is two-phase, so the evaporator duty is m_dot (h_1 - h_4) with the wet h_1). The suction density is the homogeneous two-phase density 1 / (x v_g + (1 - x) v_l) – it is what sets the swept mass flow – and the isentropic discharge state is the isentropic compression of that two-phase state. Used by the discharge-temperature control of tmhp.compressor_envelope.

Note

Whether a given heat exchanger acts as the evaporator or the condenser in heating versus cooling mode is decided by the caller (_calc_state), which chooses T_evap_K and T_cond_K accordingly before calling this function.

Compressor envelope¶

Compressor operating envelope shared by every cycle-resolved heat-pump model.

A real compressor runs inside an envelope that its manufacturer publishes and its controller enforces. Five limits matter for the single-stage vapour compression cycle TMHP solves, and this module holds the rule for each so the five families (ASHP, GSHP, ASHPB, GSHPB, WSHPB) apply them identically.

Pressure ratio (PR_cycle_min / PR_cycle_max)

The physically primary lift limit is the pressure ratio PR = P_cond / P_evap, not a fixed temperature lift: the lift needed to reach a given PR maps non-linearly through the saturation curve and differs by refrigerant and level, so a fixed minimum lift (e.g. 20 K) over- or under-states the true limit; a PR floor is transferable. Scroll axial loading / built-in volume ratio, the oil-feed pressure difference and the collapse of isentropic efficiency place the floor near PR ~ 1.5 (1.3–2.0); below it the cycle is clamped onto the floor. The ceiling is the single-stage scroll’s hardware limit, about 11:1 (self-unloading of AC scrolls; Cuevas & Lebrun 2009; Bertsch & Groll 2008; Gayeski et al. 2011); above it the point is rejected (PR_ABOVE_MAX). Every family defaults to PR_CYCLE_MAX_SINGLE_STAGE. Higher ratios belong to two-stage or vapour-injection machines, which the single-stage cycle does not describe. Since the discharge-temperature limit below binds first almost everywhere, the ratio ceiling acts as a hardware sanity bound.

Discharge temperature (T_dis_max)

Lubricant breakdown and valve / motor temperature limit the gas leaving the compressor. Compressor application guides give the protection setting: Danfoss SH scrolls (R410A) “discharge gas temperature must not exceed 135 degC”; Danfoss PSH (R410A/R454B) start wet injection at 121 degC with a 135 degC maximum safety value; the external discharge-gas protection of Danfoss DSF scrolls (R32, which “leads to higher discharge temperatures than R410A”) opens at 150 degC at most. Unit controllers act earlier on the discharge pipe temperature (Daikin room units reduce frequency from about 100 degC and stop at 110–120 degC). A point whose computed discharge temperature exceeds the limit is not an operating point (DISCHARGE_TEMP_ABOVE_MAX). The defaults (DISCHARGE_TEMP_MAX_C, default_discharge_temp_max()) are the protection values – R32 150 degC, every other fluid 135 degC – not the controllers’ earlier set points, because the cycle model has no shell heat loss and puts every compressor loss into the gas (the default electro-mechanical efficiency is about 1), so its discharge temperature is an upper estimate of the machine’s.

Discharge-temperature control comes first (discharge_controlled(), x_suction_min). A unit whose discharge runs hot does not stop: its controller opens the expansion valve, so the suction superheat falls, and then lets the suction go wet or injects liquid. The cycle at a candidate operating point is therefore re-solved, compressor speed included, first with the suction superheat reduced from its value towards 0 K until the discharge temperature equals T_dis_max, then – if 0 K is not enough – with a two-phase suction of quality x lowered towards x_suction_min (default X_SUCTION_MIN_DEFAULT = 0.9). Wet suction is modelled as a homogeneous two-phase state at the evaporating pressure (see tmhp.refrigerant.calc_ref_state()); the evaporator outlet is then two-phase, the swept mass flow follows the homogeneous density, and the isentropic work is that of the two-phase state, so the cycle energy balance stays closed. The efficiency maps were fitted to dry suction, so applying them to wet suction is an approximation. Only a point that is still above the limit at x_suction_min is outside the envelope. x_suction_min=1 keeps the superheat stage only; None switches the control off.

How a family answers a point the control cannot bring inside the limit: the space-conditioning models (ASHP, GSHP) reject the candidate inside the operating-point search, so the search moves to a smaller lift if one carries the duty, and otherwise the existing capacity search returns the largest duty the envelope allows with capacity_clamped='max' and capacity_binding='discharge_temperature'. The boilers (fixed requested duty) check the solved operating point; analyze_steady then tries smaller duties (steady_envelope()) and returns the largest one inside the limit the same way, or the off state with failure_reason='discharge_temp_above_max' when even a small duty exceeds it. A dynamic boiler step does the same with its one cycle solve per step (reduce_duty_within_envelope()): it runs at the reduced duty, or is an off step with that reason when no duty fits. The boilers answer every limit a smaller duty cures this way – the pressure-ratio ceiling, the source heat exchanger’s range and the efficiency floor as well (duty_reduction_binding()); capacity_binding names the limit. The efficiency floor also has a lower side: towards low speed the shipped fit falls below it while larger duties run. A boiler’s analyze_steady answers a request refused there with the smallest duty that runs (raise_duty_within_envelope(), capacity_clamped='min'), which is then reported as the mean of on/off cycling like a request below the minimum speed.

Condensing pressure (T_cond_max)

The top of every published envelope is a maximum condensing temperature, which is a maximum high-side pressure: the shell, the scroll set and the motor are built for it, and the unit’s high-pressure switch stops the compressor just above it. T_cond_max is that limit as the saturation temperature of the condensing pressure [degC]; the check compares pressures, P_cond > P_dew(T_cond_max) (for a zeotropic blend the envelope temperature is the dew point; the cycle’s own condensing temperature is the bubble point, lower by the glide – R407C reaches the 65 degC limit at 61.2 degC). A machine with a different high-pressure rating is given its own T_cond_max; None switches the check off.

A point above the limit is not an operating point (CONDENSING_TEMP_ABOVE_MAX), and it is answered like the discharge limit below, because the condensing temperature is the demand-side temperature plus the condenser approach and the approach shrinks with the duty: the space-conditioning models reject the candidate inside the operating-point search, the boilers reduce the duty (reduce_duty_within_envelope()) and report capacity_clamped='max' with capacity_binding='condensing_temperature'. The boundary duty is found by a secant on the condensing temperature (_duty_on_condensing_limit(): three cycle solves in all, instead of the ladder and bisection of the other limits), so a dynamic step that runs against the limit stays cheap. When the demand side is itself at the limit (a tank at T_cond_max) no duty runs: the off state with failure_reason='condensing_temp_above_max'.

A boiler’s thermostat is told where the envelope ends (limit_tank_setpoints_to_envelope()): an upper set point above the tank temperature at which the nameplate duty condenses on the limit (condensing_tank_cutout()) is capped there at construction, with a UserWarning. A dynamic step solves one cycle and applies its heat over the step, so without the cap a long step overran the limit and the temperature at which the heat pump stopped depended on the step.

Speed (rps_min / rps_max)

rps_min is the inverter’s floor (15 rev/s). rps_max defaults to default_rps_max() of the rated speed the model solves at its rating point: twice the rated speed, at most RPS_MAX_ABSOLUTE, at least 1.5 times the rated speed. The rated speed itself is solved over [rps_min, RATED_SPEED_SEARCH_MAX].

Efficiency domain

The default efficiency fits are guarded outside their (PR, N) data (tmhp.compressor_efficiency). Steady results carry efficiency_extrapolated so a caller can see a point evaluated outside the fitted data. At low speed and high pressure ratio the guard’s first-order continuation reaches eta_is 0.14; no separate speed floor is imposed there because every such point already exceeds the discharge limit (85 of 85 running points of the 2026-11-13 sweep, at 191–348 degC).

Compressor reference state¶

Compressor reference state solved from nominal capacity.

BITZER compressor efficiencies use absolute shaft speed in rev/s. Models normalise the ground heat exchanger refrigerant-side UA by a rated mass flow (m_dot_ref / m_dot_ref_rated). Both denominators belong to one physical point – the reference state – and this module computes that point from the same inputs every model already requires:

hp_capacity -> V_cmp_ref -> rating condition -> rps_rated -> m_dot_ref_rated
V_cmp_ref

Swept displacement. When the caller gives none, the models use tmhp.compressor_speed.default_displacement(), a capacity-scaled reduced-order default, not an identification of a real compressor.

rating condition

A standard (or manufacturer/user) rating point for the model family: mode, source- and load-side inlet temperatures, the model’s reference secondary flows and its rated UA values. It is independent of whatever operating condition is simulated afterwards.

rps_rated

The speed at which the model delivers hp_capacity at the rating condition, with every efficiency evaluated at that actual shaft speed.

m_dot_ref_rated

The refrigerant mass flow of that same state.

Capacity-only initialization constructs a representative compressor; it does not identify the exact manufacturer compressor.

Solution method¶

At the rating condition the duty on the rated side (load side) is known, so the rated-side saturation temperature follows from the rated-UA heat exchanger directly. For a trial duty on the other side, the other saturation temperature follows the same way; the cycle at those two temperatures then fixes the speed through the bounded root

\[Q_{model}(rps) - Q_{rated} = 0\]

with efficiencies evaluated at the actual bounded shaft speed (or the caller’s own callables). Heating is parameterized by speed, closing source mass flow first and then the rated condenser duty; this does not assume a monotone condenser-duty curve. Variable ground-HX UA is not used here – it needs m_dot_ref_rated, which is what is being solved for – so both heat exchangers use their rated UA at their reference flow.

A rated capacity that needs a speed outside [rps_min, rps_max] – or a rating point the subcritical cycle or the rated heat exchangers cannot reach – is reported as REFERENCE_CAPACITY_INCONSISTENT instead of being clamped onto a bound: a clamped speed would deliver a different capacity and silently redefine the machine. The pressure-ratio envelope is an operating limit and is not imposed on the rating point; its pressure ratio is reported.

source_T_C / load_T_C are the secondary-fluid inlet temperatures. TMHP’s air coils are dry (sensible only), so wet-bulb conditions of the standards are not used. class tmhp.reference_state.HXSide(fluid, T_in_C, UA, volume_flow=None, rho_cp=None)[source]¶

Bases: object

Secondary side of one refrigerant heat exchanger at the rating point.

fluid is "air" or "water" for a single-phase stream crossing a constant-saturation-temperature surface (Q = eps C dT, eps = 1 - exp(-UA / C)), or "tank" for the boiler condenser immersed in a lumped tank (Q = UA dT), as in the models’ own cycles.

rho_cp is the stream’s volumetric heat capacity rho c_p [J/(m³ K)]. Left out, the stream is standard air or water (the constants of tmhp.constants). A source loop filled with an antifreeze mixture is a "water" (liquid) side that carries the mixture’s own value – SourceFluid.rho * SourceFluid.cp, what the model’s cycle uses on that side – so that the reference state and the cycle solve the rating point with one heat capacity rate. (The default 25 % ethylene glycol holds 8 % less heat per litre than water.)
