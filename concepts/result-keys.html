Result keys¶

analyze_steady returns a flat dict and analyze_dynamic a pandas.DataFrame whose columns are the same keys, one row per time step. This page lists every key the five released families return with their default options: what it means, its unit and sign, and which family carries it. The model pages explain the physics behind a group of keys; this page is the index.

How to read a result¶

  • The unit is in the key. E_cmp [W] is in watts, T_tank_w [°C] in degrees Celsius, dV_ou_a [m3/s] in cubic metres per second. A key without a bracket is a flag (bool), a name (str) or a plain ratio.

  • Everything is a rate. Heat and power are in watts, never joules. In a dynamic row they are means over the step, so energy is the column times dt_s.

  • Location, not role. Heat duties are named after the heat exchanger they cross (Q_ref_iu, Q_ref_ou, Q_ref_tank, Q_ref_ground) and are never negative; mode says which of them is the condenser.

  • The request is signed, the result is not. Q_r_iu < 0 asks for heating and Q_r_iu > 0 for cooling; Q_request [W] repeats it as given, Q_ref_iu [W] is the magnitude delivered.

  • Off is 0, undefined is NaN. An off or failed row has hp_is_on == False, zero heat and power, and NaN for what does not exist without a running cycle (COP, refrigerant states, efficiencies). failure_reason separates “not asked to run” ("none") from “could not run” (failure_reason semantics).

  • A row can be a time average. Below the minimum compressor speed a steady result is the mean of on/off operation, and a boiler’s dynamic step is the mean over a step in which the thermostat may have switched the compressor. runtime_fraction [-] is below 1 on such rows: rates are means, temperatures, pressures and speed are those of the running machine.

In the Where column a family name alone means the key is in both analyze_steady and analyze_dynamic; (s) marks steady only, (d) dynamic only; all is all five families in both. The families are ASHP (AirSourceHeatPump), GSHP (GroundSourceHeatPump), ASHPB (AirSourceHeatPumpBoiler), GSHPB (GroundSourceHeatPumpBoiler) and WSHPB (WaterSourceHeatPumpBoiler).

from tmhp import AirSourceHeatPump

row = AirSourceHeatPump().analyze_steady(Q_r_iu=-9_000, T0=-15.0, T_a_room=20.0)

row["failure_reason"]      # "none": the unit runs
row["capacity_clamped"]    # "max": it delivers less than the 9 kW asked for
row["capacity_binding"]    # "compressor": the speed ceiling stops it
row["Q_ref_iu [W]"]        # heat delivered, about 3.8 kW
row["Q_unmet [W]"]         # the rest of the request, about 5.2 kW

Status and flags¶

Keys without a unit bracket are flags or names. Read these first: they say whether the numbers of the row are an operating point, a reduced one, a time average, or placeholders.

Key

Where

Meaning

hp_is_on

all

True when the compressor runs in this result (for a dynamic step: during any part of it).

mode

all

"heating", "cooling" or "off". The boilers only heat.

converged

all

The operating point closed: heat exchangers and compressor speed. False on a failed request, together with failure_reason.

converged_rps

all

The compressor-speed solve met the duty inside rps_min … rps_max.

failure_reason

all

"none" or why a requested point has no operating point; the row then holds off-state placeholders. Values: failure_reason semantics.

capacity_clamped

all

None when the request was met (and on an off or failed row); "min" when it was below the minimum-speed duty (the row is then the on/off mean, see Request, capacity and part load; ASHP also when it was below the smallest duty the envelope’s efficiency floor admits); "max" when the unit delivers less than asked.

capacity_binding

all

On a "max" row, the limit the next larger duty runs into: "compressor" (rps_max), "discharge_temperature", "condensing_temperature", "pressure_ratio", "compressor_efficiency", "cycle" (ASHP, GSHP: a coil at the edge of the approach search), "outdoor_coil" (ASHPB), "source_heat_exchanger" (GSHPB, WSHPB), "source_fluid_freezing" (GSHP, GSHPB, WSHPB: the loop liquid at its freezing limit). None on every other row.

efficiency_extrapolated

ASHP, GSHP (s), ASHPB (s), GSHPB (s), WSHPB (s)

A default compressor-efficiency fit is read outside its data box (PR 1.50–7.15, 33.8–72.5 rev/s) at this point. Scalar and user efficiencies give False.

discharge_control

all

The discharge-temperature control acted: suction superheat reduced, then wet suction, to hold T_dis_max. See dT_ref_superheat [K] and x_ref_cmp_in [-].

pr_clamped

ASHPB

The pressure ratio was raised to the floor PR_cycle_min and the cycle solved there.

pr_floor_active

GSHP

Same condition as pr_clamped, GSHP’s name for it.

indoor_heat_direction_valid

ASHP, GSHP

The indoor air receives heat in heating and gives it up in cooling. False makes cop_sys [-] NaN.

fan_flow_min_limit

fan_flow_max_limit

ou_fan_flow_min_limit

ASHP

The outdoor fan runs at its smallest airflow.

ou_fan_flow_max_limit

ASHP

The outdoor fan runs at its largest airflow.

iu_fan_flow_min_limit

ASHP, GSHP

The indoor fan runs at its smallest airflow.

iu_fan_flow_max_limit

ASHP, GSHP

The indoor fan runs at its largest airflow.

Request, capacity and part load¶

What was asked for, what was delivered, and how a request outside the modulation range was answered. runtime_fraction [-] is the one key that tells a time-averaged row from a continuous one.

Key

Where

Meaning

Q_request [W]

all

The duty asked for, with the sign it was given (Q_r_iu: negative heating, positive cooling; boilers: positive), on every row. A boiler’s analyze_steady: the Q_ref_tank argument. A boiler’s dynamic step: hp_capacity when the thermostat called the compressor during the step (whether it ran or failed), 0 when it did not.

Q_unmet [W]

ASHP, GSHP

Part of the request not delivered, ≥ 0. Sum it times the time step for a schedule’s unmet energy.

Q_max [W]

all

On a "max" row, the duty delivered (the ceiling); in a boiler’s dynamic step the running machine’s, not the step average (Q_ref_tank [W] is Q_max [W] times runtime_fraction [-]). NaN on every other row.

Q_min [W]

ASHP, GSHP, ASHPB (s), GSHPB (s), WSHPB (s)

On a "min" row, the duty of continuous operation at the minimum speed. NaN otherwise.

Q_ref_tank_request [W]

ASHPB

ASHPB: the Q_ref_tank argument, on every row (dynamic: as Q_request [W]). Compare with Q_ref_tank [W] on a "max" row.

Q_tank_load [W]

GSHPB, WSHPB

GSHPB, WSHPB: the tank heat the cycle was asked for (the Q_ref_tank argument).

PLR_request [-]

ASHPB (s)

Requested duty over hp_capacity, on every row.

PLR_delivered [-]

ASHPB (s)

Delivered duty over hp_capacity (0 on an off or failed row).

capacity_ratio [-]

ASHP, GSHP, ASHPB (s), GSHPB (s), WSHPB (s)

Request over the minimum-speed duty (the CR of EN 14825); 1 on a row that does not cycle, 0 when off.

part_load_factor [-]

ASHP, GSHP, ASHPB (s), GSHPB (s), WSHPB (s)

Mean COP over the continuous minimum-speed COP on a cycling row (EN 14825 degradation); 1 otherwise.

runtime_fraction [-]

all

Share of the time the compressor runs. Steady: 1, or below 1 on a "min" row. Boiler dynamic steps: the part of the step the thermostat kept the compressor on. Heat, flow and power of such a row are means over the whole period; temperatures, pressures and speed are those of the running machine.

hp_starts [-]

ASHPB (d), GSHPB (d), WSHPB (d)

Number of compressor starts inside the step (boiler dynamic steps).

hp_stop_reason

GSHPB (d), WSHPB (d)

"condensing_limit" on a step in which the compressor envelope stopped the run, or kept it at the duty that holds the water around the coil at the envelope stop, while the thermostat called (or a minimum run time held it): the tank temperature at which the nameplate duty would condense above T_cond_max. It is a normal end of the run, not a failure (failure_reason stays "none"); the row’s heat and power are the step means. "boiling_limit" when the 100 °C cut-out did so. None on every other step.

E_cycling_loss [W]

ASHP, GSHP, ASHPB (s), GSHPB (s), WSHPB (s)

Mean electrical power drawn beyond capacity_ratio times the continuous power: the cost of on/off operation. 0 on a row that does not cycle.

Heat rates¶

Q_ref_* are refrigerant-side duties named by where the heat exchanger is, always ≥ 0: in heating the indoor unit or tank is the condenser, in cooling the indoor unit is the evaporator. Stream keys (Q_a_*, Q_tank_w_*, Q_mix_*) are enthalpy flows ṁ c (T − T0) counted from the outdoor temperature of the row and can be negative; only their differences are heat rates.

Key

Where

Meaning

Q_ref_iu [W]

ASHP, GSHP

Indoor-coil duty: heat delivered in heating, heat removed in cooling (sensible, before indoor-fan heat).

Q_ref_ou [W]

ASHP, ASHPB

Outdoor-coil duty: heat absorbed from outdoor air in heating (and by the boiler), rejected to it in cooling.

Q_ref_tank [W]

ASHPB, GSHPB, WSHPB

Heat the condenser delivers to the tank water.

Q_ref_ground [W]

GSHP, GSHPB

Duty of the heat exchanger between refrigerant and ground-loop fluid: absorbed in heating, rejected in cooling.

Q_ref_water [W]

WSHPB

Duty of the evaporator on the source-water loop.

Q_bhe [W]

GSHP, GSHPB, WSHPB

Heat taken from the ground (or source water) by the loop: positive when extracted, negative when rejected in cooling. Differs from Q_ref_ground/Q_ref_water by the pump heat E_pmp.

Q_ou_a [W]

ASHPB

Heat the outdoor air gives up across the coil (air-side counterpart of Q_ref_ou).

Q_a_iu_in [W]

ASHP, GSHP

Enthalpy flow of the air entering the indoor unit, from T0.

Q_a_iu_out [W]

ASHP, GSHP

Enthalpy flow of the air leaving the indoor unit (after the fan), from T0. Q_a_iu_out − Q_a_iu_in is the heat the room receives (negative in cooling).

Q_tank_loss [W]

ASHPB, GSHPB, WSHPB

Heat lost through the tank wall to its surroundings (T_sur); 0 in analyze_steady.

Q_tank_w_in [W]

ASHPB

Enthalpy flow of the make-up water entering the tank, from T0.

Q_tank_w_out [W]

ASHPB, GSHPB (d), WSHPB (d)

Enthalpy flow of the hot water leaving the tank, from T0.

Q_mix_sup_w_in [W]

ASHPB

Enthalpy flow of the mains water entering the mixing valve, from T0.

Q_mix_w_out [W]

ASHPB

Enthalpy flow of the mixed water delivered to the tap, from T0.

Q_dhw_request [W]

ASHPB (d), GSHPB (d), WSHPB (d)

Hot-water heat the step was asked for: scheduled draw at the set point T_mix_w_out, counted from the mains temperature.

Q_dhw_unmet [W]

ASHPB (d), GSHPB (d), WSHPB (d)

Part of Q_dhw_request not delivered, ≥ 0 (water left below the set point, or a level-varying tank cut the draw).

Electrical power and COP¶

All E_* are electrical input, ≥ 0, and 0 on an off row. COPs are NaN on an off or failed row, never 0.

Key

Where

Meaning

E_cmp [W]

all

Compressor electrical input (refrigerant work over the electro-mechanical efficiency; on a cycling or frosting row, the mean including that loss).

E_cmp_ref [W]

GSHP

Work the compressor does on the refrigerant, ṁ (h_cmp_out − h_cmp_in).

E_cmp_loss [W]

GSHP

E_cmp − E_cmp_ref: electro-mechanical loss, plus the on/off loss on a cycling row.

E_ou_fan [W]

ASHP, ASHPB

Outdoor-fan electrical input.

E_iu_fan [W]

ASHP, GSHP

Indoor-fan electrical input. Its heat is in the air leaving the indoor unit.

E_pmp [W]

GSHP, GSHPB, WSHPB

Source-loop pump electrical input. It warms the loop fluid.

E_cmp_plus_pmp [W]

GSHP, GSHPB

E_cmp + E_pmp.

E_tot [W]

all

Whole electrical input of the row: compressor plus the family’s fans or pump (and subsystem loads in composed variants).

cop_ref [-]

all

Refrigerant-side COP: the delivered duty (Q_ref_iu or Q_ref_tank) over E_cmp.

cop_sys [-]

all

System COP. Boilers: Q_ref_tank / E_tot. ASHP, GSHP: heat the room air receives or gives up, |Q_a_iu_out − Q_a_iu_in|, over E_tot (indoor-fan heat counted once, on the air).

Refrigerant state points¶

Every family reports the same seven points of the cycle, in analyze_steady and in every dynamic row. An off or failed row carries NaN. For a zeotropic blend the condenser’s dew and bubble points differ by the glide (R407C: about 4.5 K; R410A: 0.1 K). Every family reports the dew point under T_ref_cond_sat_v and the bubble point, which the cycle condenses at, under T_ref_cond_sat_l. The condensing limit T_cond_max is a dew-point temperature (tmhp.compressor_envelope), and the condenser-heat exergy (a boiler’s X_ref_tank [W]; X_ref_iu [W], X_ref_ou [W] or X_ref_ground [W] of ASHP and GSHP, whichever coil condenses) uses the bubble point. For a pure refrigerant the two keys are equal. T_ref_evap_sat is the saturated-vapour (dew) temperature of the evaporating pressure, the pressure the cycle is fixed at; a blend enters the evaporator colder than that (T_ref_exp_out), by the part of the glide it still has to evaporate through.

Exergy¶

Exergy columns are added by post-processing, with the row’s T0 [°C] as the dead state. analyze_dynamic adds them in every family; analyze_steady adds them for ASHP only (postprocess=True, the default). X_* is an exergy flow or the exergy of a heat or power, Xc_* the exergy consumed in a component, X_in_*/X_out_* what enters and leaves it, and X_eff_* its exergy efficiency 1 − Xc / X_in (system and cycle: product over input). Rows without a running cycle carry NaN or 0.

Keys that appear only in some results¶

These are not in every default result. Composed variants (solar thermal, PV and storage, UV lamp) add their own columns, described on Air-source heat pump boiler (ASHPB) and Ground-source heat pump boiler (GSHPB).

A result has the same keys on every row and in every run of the same machine, in every family: what does not apply to a row is None or NaN (capacity_binding and Q_max [W] away from a ceiling, the compressor efficiencies of an off row, GSHP’s approach keys of a point that was not solved). In a dynamic frame a name that does not apply reads as missing (isna()). Two things still depend on how the result was asked for, not on what the point did: the exergy columns (X_*) of ASHP and GSHP are written by every dynamic run and by ASHP’s analyze_steady(postprocess=True), and the diagnostics of GSHP’s coupled ground-flow solver are on that solver’s rows only (below).

Checking a result in code¶

ok = (df["failure_reason"] == "none") & df["converged"]
running = df["hp_is_on"]
averaged = df["runtime_fraction [-]"] < 1
electricity_kWh = (df["E_tot [W]"] * dt_s).sum() / 3.6e6

These four columns are in every frame of every family. A row that delivered less than asked has capacity_clamped == "max", in a steady result and in a dynamic frame of every family.

A new key added to a model has to be added here as well: tests/test_docs_alignment.py runs each family and fails when a result carries a key this page does not name.
