Water-source heat pump boiler (WSHPB)¶

Source side is a water loop with a prescribed inlet temperature; sink side is the shared DHW tank demand model used by the boiler families.

Overview¶

The class is tmhp.WaterSourceHeatPumpBoiler. Unlike GSHPB, WSHPB takes the source-side inlet temperature as a schedule input rather than computing it from a borehole field — useful when the water loop is driven by an external simulation or measurement.

Base usage¶

from tmhp import WaterSourceHeatPumpBoiler

wshpb = WaterSourceHeatPumpBoiler(ref="R134a")

result = wshpb.analyze_steady(
    T_tank_w=55.0,
    T_source=15.0,     # water-loop inlet [°C]
    Q_ref_tank=8_000,
)

Default sizing¶

As for Ground-source heat pump boiler (GSHPB), hp_capacity (default 8 kW) and the refrigerant are enough: UA_tank_hx = hp_capacity / 5, UA_water = 0.18 × hp_capacity, source-loop flow dV_b_f_lpm = 0.75 × hp_capacity / (ρ c · 3 K) (3.58 L/min per kW) and E_pmp = 75 W/ton × hp_capacity (21.3 W per kW). The rated cycle is then the same at every capacity (W10/W35: COP of the cycle 5.5 with the default refrigerant, R290, and the default efficiency map). The submerged source-water coil is H_b metres long: with none of N_1, N_2, H_b given it is 200 m, or as long as the rated source-side heat (0.75 × hp_capacity) needs at 50 W/m. Values you pass are used as given; the constructor warns (UserWarning) for a coil above 80 W/m, a laminar default flow, and a T_tank_w_upper_bound whose condensing temperature (set point + hp_capacity / UA_tank_hx) is at or above the refrigerant’s critical temperature. The condensing limit of the compressor envelope (T_cond_max) acts as in Ground-source heat pump boiler (GSHPB): an upper set point above the tank temperature it allows at the nameplate duty is capped there with a UserWarning, and analyze_steady against a hotter tank returns a reduced duty (capacity_binding == "condensing_temperature") or the off state with failure_reason == "condensing_temp_above_max".

The cycle closures are those of Ground-source heat pump boiler (GSHPB): the suction superheat is capped at the evaporator approach less dT_hx_min, and on a compressor speed bound the tank heat exchanger is solved against the heat actually delivered (dT_ref_tank [K], tank_hx_residual [W]).

analyze_steady reports a request below the minimum-speed duty as the mean of on/off cycling, exactly as Ground-source heat pump boiler (GSHPB) does (Steady state below the minimum capacity): Q_ref_tank [W] equals the request, runtime_fraction [-] is the share of the time the compressor runs, and E_cmp, E_pmp and E_tot carry the EN 14825 degradation for a unit delivering to water (Cd_cycling, the standard’s Cc, 0.9 by default; None returns the continuous minimum-speed point). The default machine at 10 °C source water and a 35 °C tank delivers 2.4 kW at its minimum speed; a 400 W request returns 400 W at 147 W of input (system COP 2.73 against 4.08 for the running machine). Dynamic runs are not affected.

Loop liquid and its freezing limit¶

source_fluid is the liquid circulating between the heat pump and the coil in the source water: "water" (default) or an antifreeze mixture such as "MPG-25%". The rule is that of Ground-loop liquid and its freezing limit on the GSHPB page: the loop liquid is never colder than its freezing point plus 3 K, i.e. 3 °C for water. With the default 3 K loop temperature difference a water loop therefore delivers its full duty down to about 6 °C entering the heat pump; below that the duty is reduced to keep the leaving water at 3 °C (capacity_binding == "source_fluid_freezing"), and a loop entering below 3 °C does not run (failure_reason == "source_fluid_freezing"). The W10/W35 rating point (leaving at 7 °C) is unaffected. For winter surface water near 0 °C fill the coil with a mixture:

wshpb = WaterSourceHeatPumpBoiler(source_fluid="MPG-25%")   # limit -6.8 °C
wshpb.analyze_steady(T_tank_w=45, T_source=1.0, Q_ref_tank=8_000)

Dynamic operation¶

analyze_dynamic follows the tank thermostat (T_tank_w_lower_bound / T_tank_w_upper_bound, 55 / 65 °C) inside each step: a step reports the fraction of it the compressor ran (runtime_fraction [-]), its starts (hp_starts [-]), and heat, power and source-water heat averaged over the step. min_on_time_s / min_off_time_s (default 0) hold the compressor to a minimum run and rest time. See Time step and the thermostat.

T_source_w_schedule is the source-water temperature of each step (default: the constructor’s Ts at every step); the row reports it as Ts [°C] and T_bhe [°C]. The cycle of a step is solved against the fluid the coil returns while it carries that step’s own extraction from that step’s water, so in every running row T_ref_evap_sat < T_bhe_f_out_running < Ts. (Earlier releases fed the evaporator the previous step’s fluid, and the first step the constructor’s Ts.) An off row has no refrigerant state: the cycle state-point columns are NaN.

Source-side mechanics¶

A single ε-NTU heat exchanger between the refrigerant evaporator and the source-side water loop. No borehole transient — in analyze_steady the loop inlet temperature is whatever the user supplies (T_source).

In analyze_dynamic the loop runs through a coil submerged in the source water. A pipe of length H_b and per-length resistance R_b in water of uniform temperature is a single-stream heat exchanger, Q = ε ṁc (T_water − T_in) with ε = 1 − exp(−H_b / (R_b ṁc)), so the fluid returns at T_in + Q / ṁc and never warmer than the water it takes heat from (0.4 K below it for the default 8 kW machine, NTU 2.2).

Sink-side mechanics¶

Shared DHW tank sink with an implicit per-step energy balance.

Every step reports the hot-water service: Q_dhw_request [W] is the scheduled draw at the set point T_mix_w_out counted from the mains temperature, Q_dhw_unmet [W] the part not delivered because the water left the mixing valve below the set point (the tank was colder than it) or a level-varying tank cut the draw. analyze_dynamic warns once (tmhp.dhw.DHWUnmetWarning) when a run leaves demand unmet; see Was the hot water delivered? for the run totals.

A step on which the thermostat called but the compressor could not run (an off row with a failure_reason), or ran below the nameplate duty (capacity_clamped == "max"), is not reported while the run goes on. analyze_dynamic ends with one tmhp.OperatingLimitWarning that counts such steps by limit and says what they did to the tank, and the same totals are in df.attrs["operating_limits"]; analyze_steady warns once about a point that does not run or runs reduced (Being told: OperatingLimitWarning).

A tank whose level varies (tank_always_full=False or prevent_simultaneous_flow) follows the rules of the air-source boiler (Air-source heat pump boiler (ASHPB), Sink-side mechanics): the draw stops at TANK_LEVEL_MIN, the make-up at full, the reported level and tank flows are the ones the step’s energy balance was solved with, and the demand the tank could not supply is dV_mix_w_out_unmet [m3/s]. Such a run starts with tank_level_init of at least TANK_LEVEL_MIN.

API reference¶

Water source heat pump boiler — physics-based cycle model.

Resolves a vapour-compression refrigerant cycle coupled to a surface-water source (e.g. river) on the evaporator side and a lumped-capacitance hot-water tank on the condenser side. At each time step the model finds the minimum-power operating point via 1D Brent optimization over the evaporator approach temperature difference, while the condenser temperature is solved analytically.

The water source is treated as an infinite-capacity thermal boundary: the evaporator draws heat from the source-water stream through an NTU-based heat exchanger without depleting it, so no long-term source temperature drift is tracked. (This distinguishes the model from the ground source variant, which resolves borehole g-functions.)

Water source heat pump boiler with surface-water source and lumped-tank model.

The refrigerant cycle is resolved via CoolProp with user-specified superheat / subcool margins. An optimizer minimises total cycle electrical input subject to NTU-based evaporator constraints against the source-water temperature and analytical condenser temperature relations.

hp_capacity and ref are enough to build a machine; the sizes left as None follow the capacity (tmhp._source_boiler), so the rated cycle is the same at every capacity: UA_tank_hx = hp_capacity / 5, UA_water = 0.18 * hp_capacity, source-loop flow dV_b_f_lpm = 0.75 * hp_capacity / (rho c * 3 K) (3.58 L/min per kW), E_pmp = 75 W/ton * hp_capacity (21.3 W per kW), and a submerged coil H_b of 200 m or as long as the rated source-side heat needs at 50 W/m. A value the caller gives is used as given. UserWarning is raised for a caller’s coil above 80 W/m, for a laminar default flow, and for an upper tank set point whose condensing temperature is at or above the refrigerant’s critical temperature.

In every running result the suction superheat is at most the evaporator approach less dT_hx_min; on a compressor speed bound the tank heat exchanger is closed on the heat actually delivered (dT_ref_tank [K], tank_hx_residual [W]). In analyze_dynamic the cycle of a step is solved against the fluid the coil returns from that step’s source water (T_source_w_schedule) while it carries that step’s own extraction.

source_fluid names the liquid circulating in the coil (tmhp.source_fluid): "water" (default) or an antifreeze mixture such as "MPG-25%". The loop liquid is never colder than its operating limit, the freezing point plus 3 K (3 °C for water): a duty that would chill it further is reduced to the largest one that does not (capacity_binding == "source_fluid_freezing"), and when no duty does the compressor stays off with failure_reason == "source_fluid_freezing". Source water colder than about 6 °C at full duty therefore needs an antifreeze mixture in the coil.

Below the minimum capacity. analyze_steady reports a request below what the compressor delivers at rps_min as the mean of on/off cycling (capacity_clamped == "min", tmhp.cycling): Q_ref_tank [W] equals the request, runtime_fraction [-] is the share of the time the compressor runs, and the electrical input carries the EN 14825 degradation of a unit delivering to water – Cd_cycling is its coefficient Cc (0.9: while the compressor is off the unit draws a tenth of its running input; 1.0: nothing; a tested unit has Cc = 1 - P_off / P_on). Cd_cycling=None returns the continuous minimum-speed point, which delivers more than the request. Dynamic runs are not affected: there the tank thermostat switches the compressor, which runs at hp_capacity for runtime_fraction [-] of a step.

analyze_steady(T_tank_w, T_source, Q_ref_tank, T0=0.0, *, return_dict=True)[source]¶

Run a steady-state performance snapshot.

Evaluates the refrigerant cycle at a given operating point (T_tank_w, T_source, Q_ref_tank) without solving the tank energy balance or tracking dynamic flows.

Parameters:

  • T_tank_w (float) – Tank water temperature [°C] — treated as a given input.

  • T_source (float) – Source fluid temperature entering the heat pump [°C].

  • Q_ref_tank (float) – Target condenser heat rate [W].

  • T0 (float) – Dead-state / outdoor-air temperature [°C] (for exergy calculations).

  • return_dict (bool) – If True return dict; else single-row DataFrame.

Returns:

Cycle state plus diagnostic flags. Notable keys:

  • "converged" (bool) — True only when the evaporator approach closed the source-water heat exchanger and the cycle is an operating point.

  • "failure_reason" (str) — "none" for a solved point; otherwise "cycle_invalid" (no cycle, or a cycle that is not an operating point, see tmhp.dynamic_context.unphysical_cycle_detail()), "pr_above_max" (pressure-ratio ceiling), "discharge_temp_above_max" (even a reduced duty exceeds T_dis_max; a request above the limit that a smaller duty meets returns that duty with capacity_clamped="max" and capacity_binding="discharge_temperature"), "condensing_temp_above_max" (the same for the condensing limit T_cond_max: the tank is too hot for any duty; a reduced duty reports capacity_binding="condensing_temperature"), "hx_not_converged" (the source-water heat exchanger cannot carry the duty at any approach), "optimizer_failed" or "source_fluid_freezing" (the loop liquid would run below its operating limit, 3 °C for water, at every duty; a request that a smaller duty keeps on the limit returns that duty with capacity_clamped="max" and capacity_binding="source_fluid_freezing"; see tmhp.source_fluid).

  • "failure_detail" (str, failures only) — which check rejected a "cycle_invalid" point.

  • "capacity_clamped", "capacity_binding", "Q_max [W]" and "Q_request [W]" are on every row (None or NaN where they do not apply; "Q_request [W]" is the Q_ref_tank asked for), and an off or failed row carries every key of a running one (NaN where it has no value): the keys do not depend on the point. "T_ref_cond_sat_v [°C]" is the dew point of the condensing pressure, above "T_ref_cond_sat_l [°C]" by the glide for a zeotropic blend.

  • "runtime_fraction [-]", "capacity_ratio [-]", "part_load_factor [-]", "Q_min [W]" and "E_cycling_loss [W]" — on every row. A request below the duty at rps_min (capacity_clamped == "min") is reported as the mean of on/off cycling: Q_ref_tank [W] equals the request (Q_min [W] is the continuous duty), heat and refrigerant rates are the running machine’s times capacity_ratio [-] – the compressor runs that share of the time, runtime_fraction [-] – and the electrical inputs (compressor and source pump) carry the EN 14825 degradation for a unit delivering to water (Cd_cycling: the running values times capacity_ratio / part_load_factor), so both COPs are the continuous ones times the part-load factor. Q_bhe [W] is the mean source-side heat less that pump power. Temperatures, pressures and speed are those of the running machine. With Cd_cycling=None the row is the continuous minimum-speed point and Q_ref_tank [W] exceeds the request.

Every reason other than "none" comes with an off-mode state: Q_ref_tank [W] = E_cmp [W] = 0 and NaN COP. No unsolved point is returned with cycle numbers.

Return type:

dict | DataFrame

Warns:

Issued once per model and limit unless the "always" filter is set (tmhp.operating_limits).
